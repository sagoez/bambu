## Filling this in

For each row, add the URL you downloaded from and the licence stated on that
page. Most Printables / MakerWorld / Thingiverse models are Creative Commons,
but the variants differ in whether commercial use and redistribution are
allowed - and redistribution is exactly what committing them here does.

If a licence turns out to forbid redistribution, move that folder into
`reference/` instead. It stays on disk and drops out of the repo.
